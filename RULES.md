# RAHEE — Rules

---

## 1. The game in one paragraph

2–6 players, ~40 minutes. Each player is a **rahee** (traveller) with a character, a starting city, and six destination goals. Your **starting hand of playing cards is your entire itinerary** — nothing is ever drawn or replaced after setup, except for the specific Ace setup rule described below. You spend those cards to move across a map of India, paying ₹200 per step out of a ₹20,000 budget, and score by visiting tourist places, completing your character's destinations, and collecting sets of themed memories. The game is short and tight: 6–7 cards each means 6–7 turns each.

## 2. Components

- **Board** — map of India. Cities are dots joined by route lines. 51 cities are numbered — those are the **tourist places** and each has a matching stamp in the right-hand column.
- **Deck** — one standard deck of playing cards, with 7s, 8s and 9s removed.
- **Tokens** — minimum 2 per player: one on the map, one on the character card. More for marking tourist stamps.

### Card movement values

| Card | A | 2–6 | 10 | J | Q | K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Steps | 1 | face value | 10 | 10 | 5 | 5 |

### The four memory suits

| Suit | Memory |
|---|---|
| ♠ Spades | **Bazaar** — shopping |
| ♥ Hearts | **Food** — eating something new |
| ♣ Clubs | **Language** — hearing a language |
| ♦ Diamonds | **Festival** — celebrating |

### Money constants

| Item | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Starting budget | ₹20,000 |
| Travel | ₹200 per step |
| Flight surcharge | ₹4,000 (on top of step cost) |
| Kochi–Kavaratti ship surcharge | ₹1,000 (on top of step cost) |
| Sri Vijayapuram–Great Nicobar ship surcharge | ₹200 (on top of step cost) |
| Tourist visit ("Stay cost") | ₹2,000 |
| Shared tourist visit on a road trip | ₹1,000 each |
| Road-trip companion pays the driver | ₹100 per step travelled together |

Leftover money scores **nothing**. Overspending **is allowed** but penalised at scoring.

## 3. Setup

1. **Budget** — every player starts with ₹20,000.
2. **First player** — whoever travelled furthest recently. Turn order then runs **clockwise**.
3. **Character selection** — runs the *other* way: start with the player to the first player's **right** and go **anticlockwise**. Each player claims a character by putting a token on it.
4. **Place your traveller** — put a token on your character's printed starting city.
5. **Build the deck for the player count** (below), deal hands, and each player lays **one card from their dealt hand face-up** in front of them as their first **road-trip card**. The rest of the hand stays hidden.

### Deck and dealing

| Players | Cards used | Total | Dealt |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 2–4 | A, 2–6, 10 of all four suits | 28 | **one 10** + 6 more each |
| 5 | add J (10), Q (5), K (5) of all four suits | 40 | **one J or 10** + 6 more each |
| 6 | same 40-card deck | 40 | **one J or 10** + 5 more each |

Every player is **guaranteed** one high-movement card. Then the rest is shuffled and dealt. Unused cards are set aside; **no card is ever drawn after setup**, except for the replacement described by the Ace setup rule.

## 4. A turn

You play a card, then:

1. **Move** — move at least 1 step and up to the card's value along connected routes; pay travel costs.
2. **Note memories and tourism** — record memories earned, and optionally pay to visit the tourist place you ended on.
3. **Offer a road trip** — if you ended on a city containing other players, you may invite them.
4. **End your turn** — lay the card(s) you played face-up in front of you. These accumulate and become future road-trip cards.

You **cannot pass**. You must play a card and move at least one step while you still have a playable card.

### Movement details

- You may move **fewer** steps than the card's value (but never zero).
- One step = one route segment between two adjacent cities.
- A **flight** between two airports on the mainland, or a marked flight to an island, counts as **one step** — plus the ₹4,000 surcharge.
- The **Kochi–Kavaratti ship** counts as its route step(s) plus the ₹1,000 surcharge.
- The **Sri Vijayapuram–Great Nicobar ship** counts as its route step(s) plus the ₹200 surcharge.

## 5. Memories

You earn memories **of the played card's suit**. You may create more than one memory in a single turn, and the different memory rules can stack.

1. **Efficient travel** — you moved *exactly* the card's full value → 1 memory of that suit.
2. **Meeting a traveller** — you finished movement in a city where another player already was → 1 memory of that suit.
3. **Road trip** — every traveller on the trip gains 1 memory of the **road-trip card's** suit.

Memories are **free**.

A normal "Meeting a traveller" memory is earned simply by ending movement in a city where another player already is. It does not require starting a road trip.

### Road-trip ending at another player

If a road trip ends in a city containing a player who **wasn't on the trip**, that player does **not** receive the road-trip memory. The road-trip memory is awarded only to the travellers who participated in the road trip.

### Worked example from the manual

Green plays 2♦ and moves exactly two steps into Blue's city → two Festival memories: one for efficiency and one for meeting Blue.

In the road-trip example, Green ends with Blue, flips Blue's 4♦, and they move 4 steps to Bhopal. Green pays 4 × ₹200 = ₹800; Blue pays Green 4 × ₹100 = ₹400; Green's net is ₹400. Green gets **2 Festival memories** (trip + efficiency), Blue gets **1**. Each pays ₹1,000 for the Bhopal stamp.

## 6. Tourist places and destination goals

The **numbered** cities are tourist places. When you finish movement on one you **may** pay **₹2,000** to visit it and mark its stamp with your token. **Each tourist place scores only once per player** — but different players can each visit the same place.

### Destination goals

A character destination is completed only when the player **visits and marks the destination as a tourist place by paying the required ₹2,000 tourist visit cost**.

Merely ending movement on a destination city does **not** complete the destination.

Each completed character destination scores **5 points**. Reaching five completed destination goals also gives the 10-point bonus.

## 7. Road trips

**At most one road trip per turn.**

1. **Invite** — if you end movement in a city with other players, you may invite them.
2. **Choose a road-trip card** — the road-trip card must belong to **another participating player**, not the active player. That player's face-up road-trip card is turned **face-down**; its value is the trip's movement allowance, and its suit is the trip's memory suit.
3. **Travel together** — the active player proposes a route; travellers may negotiate direction. All participating tokens move together, at least 1 step, up to the card's value. Players may travel part of the way.
4. **Pay** — the active player pays the normal travel cost for the whole group's route. Each companion pays **their own** flight and ferry surcharges, **plus ₹100 per step** to the active player, offsetting their cost.
5. **Memories** — every traveller on the trip gains 1 memory of the road-trip card's suit. If the group moves **exactly** the card's full value, the active player gains **one additional** memory of that suit for efficient travel.
6. **Shared tourism** — if the trip ends in a tourist place, each traveller may visit it for **₹1,000** instead of ₹2,000.
7. **Drop-off** — a companion may leave partway. They pay ₹100 per step actually travelled, and **forfeit the shared-tourism discount**.
8. **Road-trip destination with a non-participant** — if the trip ends in a city holding a player who **wasn't** on the trip, the non-participating player does **not** receive the road-trip memory. Only the travellers who participated in the road trip receive that road-trip memory.

### Road-trip card availability

A player can participate in a road trip as the active player only if another participating player has a face-up road-trip card that can be flipped.

If you want to take a road trip with Player 1 and Player 1 has **no face-up road-trip card**, you cannot use Player 1's card for the trip. You may instead take a road trip with Player 2, Player 3, or another participating player who has a face-up road-trip card.

### Travelling together

At the **start** of your turn, if other players are in your city, you may play your **own** card and ask them to travel with you. This is **not** a road trip: no face-up card is flipped and nobody gets a road-trip memory. Companions may get off partway, pay you **₹100 per step** plus their own surcharges, and everyone still aboard at the end may visit the tourist place there for **₹1,000** each. Only you make memories (efficient travel, and an Ace's extra memory).

Because travelling together is not a road trip, you may **still take one road trip** afterwards, from where it ended, under the normal road-trip rules above (for example with a player who was already standing there). **At most one road trip per turn** still applies.

## 8. Aces

An Ace can be played in exactly one of three ways:

1. **Alone** — move one step.
2. **As a booster** — with another card, adding **+1** to its movement value. "Full value" for efficient travel then *includes* the +1.
3. **For a memory** — with another card, creating **one extra memory of either card's suit** (your choice).

If you start with two or more Aces, you may discard one from the game and draw a replacement card from the deck, if cards remain. If no cards remain, you may **request a reshuffle**.

## 9. Ending and scoring

**Trigger:** as soon as any player either **plays the last card from their hand**, or **reaches five of their character's completed destination goals**. Then **finish the current round** so everyone has taken the same number of turns.

### Scoring

| # | Points |
|---|---:|
| 1 | **10** per tourist place visited |
| 2 | **5** per character destination completed |
| 3 | **10** bonus for reaching 5 completed destinations |
| 4 | **15** per set of three memories of the same suit |
| 5 | **20** per set of four memories, one of every suit |
| 6 | **−5** per ₹500 (or part of ₹500) spent over ₹20,000 |

**Memory set rule:** a single memory may count in **one** same-suit set **and** one four-suit set. The manual's example: 3 Bazaar + 1 Festival + 1 Language + 1 Food = **35** points (15 for the Bazaar triple, 20 for the four-suit set).

## 11. Build implementation notes

The game must be **data-driven**: cities, coordinates, routes, route types, step counts, surcharges, tourist places, characters and destination goals should live in an editable data file that the engine reads at load time and renders over the supplied background plate. The engine holds **only** rules and mechanisms — no board knowledge. Adding a city or retyping a route should change the game with zero engine edits.

Minimum screens: Setup → private Handover → Board/play → Turn actions → Scoreboard. Local hotseat, 2–4 players, full rule enforcement, save/resume, transparent final score breakdown. Stretch: 5–6 players, visual map editor, greedy AI, route hints, undo.

For the UI: the board is the centrepiece, with a sidebar showing collected memories and visited places, and the current player's character destinations highlighted inside that visited list.
